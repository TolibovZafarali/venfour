## Same-day ad-preview diagnosis

After the user reported a missing preview, the exact settings were reproduced: `insurance offer too low for totaled car`, Concord, Missouri, English, mobile. Google's Results tab reported no matching keywords. The live keyword table confirmed that the exact keyword exists, is enabled, and is **Not eligible — Low search volume**. Low-volume keywords do not participate in auctions until Google reactivates them as search traffic increases; see [Google's definition](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2616014?hl=en-GB).

The published inventory remains 14 keywords: **6 Eligible and 8 Not eligible / Low search volume**. All showed zero impressions and zero clicks at this check. The ad itself showed Eligible and the campaign remained Enabled / Eligible (Limited).

The eligible keywords were `insurance undervalued my car`, `total loss offer too low` (phrase), `negotiate total loss settlement`, `dispute total loss valuation`, `insurance lowball total loss` (phrase), and `ccc valuation dispute`.

A controlled comparison using `dispute total loss valuation` with the same preview location, language and device also returned no matching keywords, despite that exact keyword showing Eligible in the table. The preview therefore did not establish why the eligible terms were not appearing. A new-campaign serving delay is a possibility, not a confirmed root cause; [Google notes that recently enabled campaigns may take time to begin serving](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9208915?hl=en).

No bids, budgets, keywords, match types or campaign settings were changed during this diagnosis. Enabling the campaign is confirmed; actual ad delivery is not yet demonstrated by the zero-impression snapshot.
