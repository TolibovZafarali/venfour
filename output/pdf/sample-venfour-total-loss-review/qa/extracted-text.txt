01
Condition: a repeated $1,100 deduction
The report gives no itemized basis for the same deduction on all three vehicles. Ask what it represents
and what evidence supports it. Repetition alone does not establish an error.
02
Mileage: sensible direction, unclear method
The insurer subtracts for lower-mileage vehicles and adds for a higher-mileage vehicle. Ask how the
amounts were determined; the report does not explain the method.
03
Market evidence: a difference worth explaining
All five asking prices on page 3 exceed the insurer's value. Ask the adjuster to address those records
and any differences that affect the comparison.
Request the basis for the deductions and an updated valuation report or written explanation. Do not add
$1,100 to the $2,400 asking-price gap: they examine the same valuation and may overlap.

03 / CUSTOMER RECONSIDERATION REQUEST
A specific request, ready to send
The client reviews the facts, recipient and attachments, then sends the request through their own email or
insurer channel. This ready-to-send wording is for the hypothetical case only.
SUBJECT
Request to review vehicle valuation - claim SAMPLE-TL-2026-01
Hello Morgan,
Thank you for your help with my total-loss claim for my 2020 Toyota RAV4 XLE AWD. I reviewed
the September 8 report and would appreciate another look at the $20,500 vehicle valuation.
The attached review includes five comparable vehicles with asking prices from $22,400 to
$23,400 and a median of $22,900. The records relate to the September 2 loss date. I understand
these are asking prices rather than verified sale prices, and vehicle differences may affect the
comparison.
Could you please explain the $1,100 condition deduction applied to each comparable and the
evidence supporting it? I reported normal pre-loss condition and no existing damage. I would
also appreciate an explanation of the mileage adjustments.
Please reconsider the vehicle valuation in light of these records. If it remains unchanged, could
you explain how the comparable vehicles were considered and which differences or adjustments
support the result? An updated valuation report or written explanation would be helpful.
Thank you for your time and help,
Jordan Lee
After the client sends it
Attach the actual case review and supporting evidence, send the request, and mark it as sent in Venfour.
Keep the sent copy. When the insurer replies, paste its response or upload the original and record any
revised vehicle-value amount.

We revised the vehicle valuation to $21,300. The condition deduction is now $300 per comparable;

Supported next action: ask for the adjustment basis. The customer chooses to continue and prepares a
follow-up using the saved evidence. This clarification does not amount to a recommendation to reject
