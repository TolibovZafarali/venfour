How the set is used
Venfour ranks vehicles by similarity, not price. This sample shows the complete comparison set: five separate
hypothetical vehicles, with no duplicates, subject-vehicle matches or supplemental listings chosen for higher
prices.
Loss-date context
Historical activity here is assumed for illustration, not verified. Venfour distinguishes loss-date records from
current listings. A current listing does not establish that a vehicle was available at that price on the loss date.
Material limitations
Asking prices are not verified sale prices. No independent dollar adjustments are made for mileage, condition,
equipment, location, certification or warranty. Condition and optional equipment may differ. These vehicles do
not represent the entire market.

04 / CONCLUSION AND ACTION
What the evidence supports
The supported conclusion is a reason to question the insurer's current vehicle valuation. The evidence does
not establish a new appraised value or a guaranteed settlement target.
Start with the same measure.
The $20,500 baseline is the insurer's vehicle valuation before separate settlement items. It is not a check after
a deductible or loan payoff.
Compare it with the recorded market evidence.
The five hypothetical asking prices run from $22,400 to $23,400, with a $22,900 median. The insurer's value is
$1,900 below the lowest asking price and $2,400 below the median.
Treat the gap as a review question.
The $2,400 median difference is about 11.7% of the insurer's $20,500 value. It shows the size of the
discrepancy worth discussing. It does not isolate an insurer error or measure the amount the customer will
recover.
Keep the adjustment questions separate.
A clearer explanation of the $1,100 condition deduction may resolve some of the difference. Removing that
deduction would not, by itself, establish the market value. Do not count it again on top of the asking-price gap.
A practical reconsideration plan
1
Send one focused request
Attach the review and comparable evidence. Ask the adjuster to reconsider the vehicle value and explain
the condition and mileage adjustments.
2
Ask for a written, itemized reply
Request an updated valuation report, or a brief explanation of why the current value is maintained and how
the listed vehicles were considered.
3
Save the reply for the next review
Keep the sent request and attachments. When the insurer responds, add its exact reply, revised report and
vehicle-value amount to the Venfour case.
A useful outcome may be a corrected value, a documented explanation or confirmation that more evidence is needed. The
customer decides how to proceed.

A clear request to the adjuster
An example of the customer-controlled reconsideration request. The wording is complete for this
hypothetical case; real claim details and attachments must be reviewed before sending.
SUBJECT
Request to review vehicle valuation - claim SAMPLE-TL-2026-01
Hello Morgan,
Thank you for your help with my total-loss claim for my 2020 Toyota RAV4 XLE AWD. I reviewed
the September 8 valuation report and would appreciate another look at the $20,500 vehicle
valuation.
The attached review includes five comparable vehicles with asking prices from $22,400 to
$23,400 and a median of $22,900. The records relate to the September 2 loss date. I understand
these are asking prices rather than verified sale prices, and differences between vehicles may
affect the comparison.
Could you please explain the $1,100 condition deduction applied to each of the three
comparables and the evidence supporting it? I reported normal pre-loss condition and no
existing damage. I would also appreciate an explanation of the mileage adjustments.
Please reconsider the vehicle valuation in light of the comparable records. If the valuation
remains unchanged, could you explain how those vehicles were considered and which
differences or adjustments support your conclusion? An updated valuation report or a written
explanation would be helpful.
Thank you for your time and help,
Jordan Lee

06 / FOLLOW-UP EXAMPLE
The insurer revises its valuation
This hypothetical continuation shows how a later insurer reply can be reviewed against the original request
and saved evidence. It does not replace or rewrite the original report.
HYPOTHETICAL INSURER REPLY / SEPTEMBER 23, 2026
R2 / Sample Insurer
We reviewed your request and revised the vehicle valuation to $21,300. We reduced the condition
deduction from $1,100 to $300 for each comparable; the mileage adjustments and equal weighting
remain unchanged. The vehicles you supplied are dealer asking prices, so we have retained our
valuation method.
What changed
R2 states a revised vehicle value of $21,300 and a lower condition deduction. Check the amount and changes
against the revised valuation report.
What the insurer addressed
The insurer reconsidered the condition adjustment and correctly distinguishes dealer asking prices from sale
prices. The reply should be acknowledged rather than treated as a rejection of every point.
What remains unresolved
R2 does not explain the remaining $300 deduction, the mileage method or how the individual market vehicles
were evaluated. The revised value remains below the saved asking-price range; that fact alone does not decide
whether to accept it.
No clear recommendation yet
The saved conclusion assessed $20,500, not the new $21,300 amount. Venfour can explain the reply and
unresolved points, but the original asking-price range does not establish whether this revised offer should
be accepted or challenged further.
Confirm the revised report and amount, then decide whether to request clarification of the remaining
adjustments. Where supported and available, Venfour can prepare a follow-up using the saved evidence. It does
not calculate a new appraised value from the reply.

Support after the first request
The customer remains in control of the insurer conversation and the decision. Venfour organizes the
evidence and helps interpret what the insurer says next.
After sending
Mark the request as sent in the case and keep a copy of what was sent. The case
moves to waiting for the insurer. Venfour does not contact the adjuster on the
customer's behalf.
When the insurer replies
Paste the response or upload the original reply, and record any revised vehicle-value
offer. Review the interpretation and check uncertain amounts against the original.
If a follow-up is
supported
Choose whether to continue, review the prepared follow-up and send it yourself. Save
subsequent replies in the case so the next review has the prior request and evidence.
When deciding to finish
The customer chooses the next step and confirms any acceptance with the insurer.
Recording a decision in Venfour does not communicate acceptance to the insurer. The
case retains its saved report, replies and history.
