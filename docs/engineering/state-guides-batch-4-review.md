## Content review and access limitations

- Nebraska's current rule index links to a legacy Secretary of State Chapter 60 endpoint that could not be retrieved. Specific deductions or deadlines found only in third-party copies were not published. The guide uses independently reviewed department guidance and current legislative text; it does not suggest that other claims rules do not exist.
- New Hampshire's current title statute includes the July 7, 2026 amendments. The guide preserves the total-loss definition in paragraph VI and does not confuse its title-application deadline with the separate insurance reconsideration period. Its evidence requirement is limited to the sources allowed by the rule. An unavailable department auto-guide PDF was not used.
- New Jersey's MVC restoration instructions and insurance guidance differ in how broadly they describe salvage applicability. The guide conditions restoration advice on the vehicle's actual title classification and directs readers to confirm model-year applicability; it does not extend one page's shorthand to every vehicle.
- New Mexico's OSI PDF returned a redirect error to the web extraction tool. The direct official URL returned HTTP 200 and the five-page PDF was downloaded and its text reviewed, including the coverage discussion and final-page dispute guidance. The divisions page supplied verified current consumer-assistance information; an intermittently rate-limited complaints endpoint was not used. The MVD manual contains an older reproduced tax statute, so the separate current Taxation and Revenue page supplies the rate. No universal appraisal entitlement, numeric salvage threshold, or replacement-loss tax exemption is inferred.
- North Carolina's direct OAH rule endpoint timed out. The department's published approved rule text was reviewed instead. The [March 2026 OAH final-determination report](https://www.oah.nc.gov/media/18045/open), page 1, identifies October 1, 2020 as the last action on .0418. Earlier proposed versions were not used. Source sections and PDF page positions were checked against the text, including the tax exception and requested written statement.
- North Dakota's current statutory compilation was used instead of an earlier bill. The damage-disclosure age provisions were not substituted for the distinct salvage-certificate section. The tax-credit time period is measured from issuance of the insurer's statement, not assumed to begin on the accident or payment date.
