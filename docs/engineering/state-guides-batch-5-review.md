Reviewed September 26, 2026 (America/Chicago). Scope: Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, and Tennessee.

Each published source destination was opened and its cited section inspected. PDF page targets refer to one-based viewer pages. ## Oregon

The consumer notice separates claim-file facts, provided evaluation, undisputed payment, ownership transfer, and conditional appraisal reimbursement. Payment without settling the value dispute is conditioned on ownership-transfer documents and does not apply to retention. The 14-day possible-sale window is calendar days. DMV's September 1, 2026 Chapter J verifies the uninsured-damage threshold of at least 80%, separate insurer-declared/taken-title paths, and the 30-day surrender period beginning when the damaged vehicle became totaled. Current SOS rule destination returned a challenge page; no additional tax/fee entitlement was inferred from inaccessible rule text or an older enforcement order.

## Pennsylvania

Verified current §62.3(d)–(e), particularly applicable sales tax and report delivery within five working days after completion. The salvage paragraph applies when the consumer keeps salvage; the title procedure is not used as a payout formula. Licensed appraisal report requirements are not conflated with a policy's appraisal-clause procedure. Appraisal-clause advice is conditional. No universal repair-cost percentage was added.

## Rhode Island

Verified active February 27, 2026 claims rule, including fair market value, itemized deductions, prohibition on reconditioning/dealer preparation, and first-party 35-calendar-day recourse measured from receipt of the claim payment. Recourse tests fair market value before deductions, with an exception for documented availability of a specific comparable including VIN/location. Public copy avoids compressing the rule's 75%–80% language into a single universal threshold. Verified current §27-10.4-1 statutory appraisal route separately; did not make it depend solely on an optional policy clause. DMV repairs/inspection text is scoped to repairable Class B, not parts-only Class A.

- [Rhode Island: Unfair Property/Casualty Claims Settlement Practices](https://rules.sos.ri.gov/regulations/part/230-20-40-2) — 230-RICR-20-40-2, §§ 2.3 and 2.8(A)–(B). Checked 2026-09-26.

## Ohio

Tax reimbursement applies to first- and third-party claimants; purchase is within 30 days of receiving the cash settlement and documentation within 33 days of that same event. Cap is the tax calculated on the settlement amount, not the settlement amount itself. The additional claim-file disclosure and 35-day recourse-notice text is first-party. Calendar-day convention and last-day holiday/weekend extension retained. Owner-retention payment/title condition kept distinct from valuation. No universal policy-appraisal entitlement asserted. The verified legacy consumer inquiry page explicitly links a separate complaint route; its inquiry form is not represented as the complaint form.

- [Ohio Administrative Code 3901-1-54](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-administrative-code/rule-3901-1-54) — Paragraphs (C)(5), (H)(7)(d), and (H)(7)(f)–(g). Checked 2026-09-26.
- [Ohio Revised Code § 4505.11](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-4505.11) — Divisions (C)(4), (E), and (F). Checked 2026-09-26.
- [Ohio Department of Insurance: Consumer Services](https://legacy.insurance.ohio.gov/Classic/ConsumServ/ConServComments.asp) — Consumer Affairs contact and Online Consumer Complaint Form link. Checked 2026-09-26.

## South Carolina

Verified pre-crash market value and claim-dependent timing in DOI FAQs. Third-party reasonable/necessary loss of use remains separate from own-policy rental coverage; stopping rental at offer is described as common guidance, not a universal deadline. SCDMV's salvage exceptions retain below 75%, no water/fire damage, value below $2,000, antique, and junk distinctions. Tax treatment was researched but no verified claim-specific tax/fee rule was established; public copy asks for an explanation without asserting an allowance. Unenacted appraisal bills were not treated as law.

- [South Carolina Department of Insurance: Auto FAQs](https://doi.sc.gov/982/FAQ-Auto-Insurance) — Questions 4, 6, and 7. Checked 2026-09-26.

## South Dakota

Verified the Division's prohibition on binding appraisal policy language and its approved mutually agreed nonbinding procedure. Betterment requires a policy definition and proof the entire vehicle's value increased; public text separates repair/replacement betterment from pre-loss condition. Statutory salvage exclusions preserve more than ten model years and gross vehicle weight rating over 16,000 pounds. The lease tax credit is conditional on the same lessor/lessee, similar vehicle, remaining term, price, and other terms; it is not described as a general purchase exemption. An old examination finding was not used to assert a current universal tax-payment rule.
