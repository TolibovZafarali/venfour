# Venfour Referral Partner Agreement

DRAFT - owner review required. Proposed revision 2026-09-16.1. Not published, offered for real signature, or effective. The business details and signatures are captured in the website; the parties are Venfour LLC and the named partner legal entity.

## 1. Parties, purpose, and acceptance

This Referral Partner Agreement is between Venfour LLC (Venfour) and the legal business entity identified in the business details above (Partner). The authorized individuals sign for their respective entities, not personally. The business details, commission terms, full agreement, and electronic signing record form this Agreement.

Partner may introduce customers to Venfour's paid total-loss valuation review service. Diminished-value services, other products, and future services are excluded unless the parties separately agree in a new written version.

Partner's signature is an offer to enter this Agreement. It becomes effective only when an authorized Venfour representative countersigns this exact version and Venfour activates the partnership. Venfour will notify Partner and provide the completed copy. No referral commissions accrue under this version before that activation. Regulated partners must also receive the separate compliance approval described below before paid referrals are enabled.

## 2. Independent relationship and customer choice

The relationship is nonexclusive. Each party is an independent contractor responsible for its own personnel, expenses, licenses, and taxes. This Agreement does not create employment, a franchise, joint venture, agency, or authority for either party to bind the other.

Customers freely choose whether to use Venfour, communicate with their insurer, accept an offer, or seek other assistance. Partner must not pressure a customer to purchase, settle, change evidence, or waive or avoid a refund. Venfour's review and conclusions remain independent. Neither party promises a higher valuation, a settlement, or any particular result.

## 3. Disclosure and permitted referrals

Before or when recommending Venfour, Partner must clearly tell the customer: "If your case qualifies, Venfour may pay our business a $50 or $75 referral commission. This does not increase your Venfour fee. You are free to choose whether to use Venfour."

The disclosure must be easy to notice and understand where the recommendation is made, including in person, online, and in messages. A hidden link or general disclosure elsewhere is not enough. Partner may use only accurate, current marketing material approved by Venfour and must stop using material Venfour reasonably identifies as inaccurate or unlawful.

Partner must not create fraudulent or self-generated referrals, purchase services for the purpose of earning a commission, impersonate customers, conceal a financial relationship, make unauthorized promises, or influence valuation inputs, evidence selection, or conclusions. Legitimate corrections to inaccurate information remain welcome. No minimum referral volume or exclusive commitment is required.

## 4. Attribution and tracking corrections

The first valid partner attribution recorded for a customer case before purchase controls. Only one partner may receive a commission for a case. Clicking a link, registering, or purchasing does not by itself earn a commission. A referral must originate through the agreed tracking method while Partner is authorized to refer customers.

Either party may report a genuine tracking error with contemporaneous supporting records. Venfour will review it promptly, consistently, and in good faith. A documented correction may recognize the valid pre-purchase referral that should have been recorded; it must preserve an audit history and must not create a referral that did not occur, replace a valid earlier referral for convenience, or give two partners credit for one case. Changes to rates or agreements cannot be used to move an existing referral to more favorable terms.

## 5. What makes a successful case

A case qualifies for a commission only when all of these conditions are met:
(a) The customer was validly attributed to Partner before purchase.
(b) The customer paid for the eligible total-loss valuation review and completed the applicable review and reconsideration process.
(c) Reliable insurer documentation establishes a final vehicle value accepted by the customer, with a qualifying vehicle-value increase strictly greater than $1,000, measured under section 6.
(d) Venfour has verified the documentation, acceptance, measurement, and successful outcome.
(e) The qualifying payment has not been refunded or reversed, no relevant payment or outcome dispute remains unresolved, and the customer is not eligible for the applicable outcome-guarantee refund, subject only to the narrow goodwill exception in section 9.

Exactly $1,000 does not qualify. An estimate, unsupported customer statement, interim offer, unfinished case, or expiration of a refund-request period does not establish success. Verification requires reliable records, not Partner's or the customer's assertion alone. Venfour will explain missing evidence or a denial sufficiently for meaningful review without disclosing private claim information unnecessarily.

This Agreement does not limit customer refund rights. In particular, the existing automatic refund when Venfour's review does not support a dispute remains available without a manual request, reconsideration, final insurer response, or manual-refund deadline. Qualifying cases are not unconditionally non-refundable.

## 6. Measuring the vehicle-value increase

The baseline is the latest genuine written insurer vehicle-value offer communicated to the customer before the paid Venfour service begins. Compare it with the final accepted insurer vehicle value using the same vehicle-value components. Venfour must verify the baseline's timing and authenticity, the final insurer documentation, and the customer's acceptance.

Exclude changes attributable solely to taxes, registration or title fees, deductibles, salvage deductions, financing or loan payoff, rental, towing, storage, injury compensation, and other non-vehicle-value items. Compare equivalent underlying vehicle values, not changes in the settlement check. If the records combine amounts or use inconsistent components, obtain clarification and reconcile them before verifying success.

Measure the increase before subtracting Venfour's service fee. It is a vehicle-value increase, not the customer's net profit. Neither a difference against an earlier superseded offer nor a change in excluded items may manufacture a qualifying increase.

## 7. Monthly commission tiers

For each Partner, successful cases 1 through 9 verified in a calendar month earn $50 each. Successful case 10 and every subsequent successful case verified that month earn $75 each. The higher rate does not apply retroactively to the first nine cases. For example, 15 successful cases in one month earn $900: nine at $50 and six at $75.

Months begin and end in America/Chicago time, including daylight saving time. Count a case in the month Venfour verifies its successful outcome, not the month of a click, registration, purchase, payment eligibility, or commission payment. Counts reset each month. Assign the sequence when verification is recorded; concurrent verifications use their recorded order.

Venfour must apply consistent verification standards and process complete evidence promptly. It must not delay verification to manipulate tiers. A documented processing or tracking error will be corrected fairly with an explanation and an audit record. Later reversals ordinarily do not reduce rates already earned in good faith on other cases; proven fraud or duplicate counting may be corrected through the dispute process.

## 8. Payment timing, onboarding, and statements

Commission payment eligibility begins on the later of verification of the successful outcome or 30 days after the customer's qualifying payment. This waiting period does not turn an unsuccessful case into a successful one or remove refund rights. All amounts are in US dollars.

Venfour will initiate payment by the 15th of the month following the month in which the commission becomes payment-eligible, or the next US banking business day if that date is not a banking business day. The receiving institution may take additional time to deliver funds. There is no minimum payout threshold.

Partner must complete appropriate payee identity and tax onboarding through a secure method designated by Venfour. Do not email bank account details or tax identification numbers. Venfour may hold payment while reasonably necessary payee information is missing or payment is legally prohibited, but must identify the issue, preserve legitimate earned commissions, and arrange payment promptly after it is resolved. Any legally required withholding will be identified.

An itemized statement will identify each case by a limited reference, its verification date and month, monthly sequence, rate, eligibility date, payment or hold status, and any documented adjustment or offset. It will not disclose the customer's private claim file. Partner is responsible for its taxes; Venfour will provide legally required reporting.

## 9. Refunds, reversals, adjustments, and goodwill

A refund required under the applicable customer policy, payment reversal, or successful chargeback invalidates the affected commission. An unresolved relevant dispute pauses payment while it is reviewed. Venfour must identify the affected case and explain the basis for any adjustment. Unaffected, undisputed commissions remain payable on schedule.

If an affected commission was already paid, Venfour may recover it through a documented offset against future commissions or a reasonable repayment request, after giving Partner notice and an opportunity for good-faith review. This Agreement does not authorize Venfour to debit Partner's bank account. A legitimate dispute about an adjustment will be reviewed before a disputed offset is applied.

A purely discretionary Venfour goodwill refund does not claw back an otherwise legitimately earned commission. This exception applies only where the case independently met every success requirement and the refund was not required by policy, law, a payment reversal, a successful chargeback, fraud, or an existing customer entitlement. Venfour will document the reason; describing a required refund as goodwill cannot make a case qualify.

No commission is forfeited merely because Partner stops referring customers, terminates this Agreement, questions a statement, or a customer exercises a protected right.

## 10. Regulated partners

Attorneys, law firms, public adjusters, insurance professionals, and other licensed or regulated partners require separate written compliance approval before any paid referrals are enabled. Partner must identify its regulated activities and relevant jurisdictions and promptly report changes.

Approval must address applicable professional, licensing, referral-fee, conflict, disclosure, and fee-sharing rules for the proposed arrangement. This Agreement alone is not that approval. Venfour may withhold or suspend paid referral access where a legal restriction or unresolved compliance question requires it, explain the reason, and work in good faith on a lawful resolution. No payment will be made where prohibited by law; this does not authorize unrelated forfeitures.

## 11. Privacy, confidentiality, and records

Each party will use confidential business information and personal information only as reasonably necessary to perform this Agreement, administer lawful referrals and payments, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Each will use reasonable safeguards and restrict access to people who need it and are subject to appropriate duties.

Partner's dashboard provides limited referral and commission information. This Agreement gives Partner no right to obtain customer claim documents, vehicle records, insurer communications, payment credentials, or another partner's records. Sharing additional customer information requires a separate lawful basis and any necessary customer permission. Neither party may sell the other's confidential information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Confidentiality does not restrict information lawfully public, already known without a duty, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source. Legally required disclosure is permitted with notice where lawful and reasonable protective steps. Each party will promptly notify the other of a material unauthorized disclosure affecting this relationship and cooperate on mitigation. Retain only records reasonably needed for legal, accounting, contract, and dispute purposes; protect retained records after termination.

## 12. Questions, corrections, and disputes

Partner may ask about a referral, verification decision, statement, or adjustment using Venfour's configured contact details shown in the application or agreement delivery email. Provide the case reference and relevant records without unnecessary customer or financial information.

The parties will acknowledge and review questions reasonably promptly, exchange relevant information lawfully, explain decisions, and attempt a good-faith resolution. Venfour will retain the evidence and reasons for corrections. Delay in raising an issue does not automatically forfeit a legitimate commission; ordinary legal limitation periods and the availability of reliable records still apply. Undisputed amounts remain payable. Neither party must waive legal remedies to use this review process.

## 13. Termination and existing referrals

Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice. Venfour may pause new referrals immediately for a reasonably supported concern about fraud, security, misleading marketing, or legal compliance, and will explain the reason to the extent lawful and allow a reasonable opportunity to respond or cure where appropriate.

Termination stops new referrals under this Agreement and ends permission to use the other party's marketing materials. Legitimate earned commissions remain payable. Valid referrals recorded before termination may still qualify later under the version that governed them, provided the ordinary eligibility conditions are met and payment is lawful. Termination is not a reason to withhold those commissions or to change their terms retroactively. Payment, corrections, confidentiality, records, and dispute obligations survive as needed to carry out this section.

## 14. Future changes and preserved versions

Material changes require a new agreement version and fresh acceptance by both parties before applying to new referrals. Posting changed terms, silently editing a signed copy, or continued use alone does not amend this Agreement. Existing referrals remain governed by their retained agreement version unless a separate, explicit, lawful agreement concerning them is reached without impairing customer rights.

Signed text, business details, commission policy, signatures, and audit records will be retained without overwriting them. Contact and payment-administration updates do not rewrite signed terms. No new service is included automatically.

## 15. Responsibilities and governing law

Each party is responsible for losses directly caused by its own breach, negligence, fraud, or unlawful conduct, subject to applicable law and reasonable mitigation. Neither assumes the other's debts or guarantees a customer's insurer outcome. Neither owes speculative lost profits or indirect or punitive damages except where applicable law requires them. These limits do not eliminate earned payment obligations, repayment of a proven overpayment, responsibility for fraud or willful misconduct, or liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

The parties will cooperate reasonably on third-party complaints about their own conduct. Neither may settle a claim in a way that admits liability for, imposes duties on, or binds the other without written consent. This Agreement contains no blanket one-sided indemnity or automatic shifting of legal fees.

Missouri law governs, without overriding mandatory law that applies to a party, customer, or regulated activity. The parties will first attempt the good-faith review in section 12; either may then use a court with proper jurisdiction. This Agreement does not require private arbitration or waive jury or class rights. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to the extent they can fairly operate. A waiver must be express and does not waive future compliance.

Contract notices may be sent through the authenticated partner account and to Partner's verified contact email. Notices to Venfour must use the configured contact details in the application or agreement delivery email. The parties must keep contact details current. If delivery fails, use another available agreed channel; a known failed delivery is not effective notice. Routine notices are effective when delivered to the designated channel; a dispute about receipt will be reviewed using available records.
